Research Directions

Smart building energy efficiency

Designing proactive energy management systems using IoT sensors and AI to optimize building load profiles. This includes implementing Peak Shaving strategies and supporting data auditing for Décret Tertiaire compliance.

icSmartGrid 2025
Comparative Evaluation of Deep Learning-Based AI Models for 24-Hour Energy Forecasting in Smart Buildings Using IoT Sensor Data
Evaluates deep learning architectures for 24-hour energy forecasting using real-world IoT sensor data.
Paper

Hybrid deep learning and time-series forecasting

Developing hybrid models that combine Akima Spline Interpolation with LSTM networks to handle irregular time-series data from smart sensors. These architectures improve short-term energy forecasting accuracy over traditional methods.

Energies 2026
Interpretable Multi-Sensor Fusion for Short-Term Energy Consumption Forecasting
Interpretable multi-sensor fusion for short-term energy consumption forecasting in smart buildings.
Paper

IoT and Edge-Cloud AI

Designing distributed Edge-Cloud AI architectures to address the latency and bandwidth inefficiencies of centralized cloud platforms. This enables faster, localized decision-making for smart building energy management.

Mobility Research
PhD International Mobility at the Free University of Bozen-Bolzano
Research conducted under Prof. Antonio Liotta on Edge-Cloud AI architectures for smart building energy management.

Anomaly detection and diagnosis

Processing real-time IoT sensor data to monitor system reliability and detect anomalies. Machine learning models identify irregular patterns and support proactive failure prediction in connected infrastructures.

Research Collaboration
AI-Based Anomaly Detection and Diagnosis
Research collaboration with Prof. Majdi Mansori at Sultan Qaboos University on anomaly detection, diagnosis, computer vision, and data science.

Computer vision and data science

Applying intelligent vision, pattern recognition, and biometrics to extract meaningful representations from data. This background supports advanced data visualization, signal processing, and spatiotemporal data analytics.

Code LLMs & program synthesis

How code models behave, and how to make them better, outside English and outside Python: dedicated model families, instruction corpora, and execution-based evaluation for low-resource settings.

LLM safety & AI in CS education

EMNLP 2026

Shows how guardrails for code assistants degrade when prompts arrive code-mixed or transliterated, and what it takes to make them robust (Findings of EMNLP 2026, to appear).
